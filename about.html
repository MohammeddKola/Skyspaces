About Sky Spaces

Sky Ventures is a leading supplier of hygiene and cleaning supplies, offering a wide range of products from the Diversey line, including Suma, Taski, and Crew. Diversey is a global leader in hygiene and cleaning solutions, and Sky Ventures is proud to partner with them to bring the best products to their customers.

Suma products are specifically designed for use in commercial kitchens and foodservice areas. They offer a range of cleaning solutions, including degreasers, oven cleaners, and dishwashing detergents.

Taski products are designed for use in a wide range of commercial settings, including hotels, hospitals, and offices. They offer floor cleaners, carpet cleaners, and glass cleaners.

Crew products are designed for industrial settings, including manufacturing facilities and warehouses, offering degreasers, disinfectants, and floor cleaners.

Sky Ventures understands the importance of maintaining a clean and hygienic environment, and they are committed to providing their customers with the best products to meet their needs.
